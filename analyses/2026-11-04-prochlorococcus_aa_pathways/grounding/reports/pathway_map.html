How Prochlorococcus amino-acid pathways meet central carbon metabolism

Each arrow is one reaction or a linear run of reactions. The badge counts how many of the 45 Prochlorococcus genomes in the KG carry every gene on that arrow (KEGG KO annotation; one gene, PSTA, counted by its Cyanorak ortholog group because its KO in the KG is a hydrogenase). Where an enzyme has known alternative forms (e.g. mdh or mqo), any one counts. Hover a badge for per-gene counts, KOs and the genomes that lack it. Pathway layout is textbook biochemistry, not KG data. A 0 means no gene with that KO is annotated, which can also be an annotation gap.
